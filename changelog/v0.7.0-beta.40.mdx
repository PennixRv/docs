Owned Codex session bindings now persist exact parent/child relationships outside
removable Channel directories. `trellis channel sessions --owner-session <id>
--json` returns unique historical descendants across projects, retries, and
Channel cleanup, with explicit tracking activation and coverage. Tracking starts
with new bindings; pre-upgrade history is not recovered.

Codex workers become ready for turns only after the supervisor persists their
session binding. Conflicting ownership, cycles, corruption, and write failures
reject the binding instead of allowing unrecorded work.
