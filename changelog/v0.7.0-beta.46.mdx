This beta adds a practical route from an idea or gap to an agreed implementation plan. Brainstorming now separates facts from user decisions, preserves evidence in the natural task artifact, grills consequential choices, and defines acceptance, boundaries, rollback, and the next action before implementation.

Review guidance now closes the loop from evidence and confidence to ownership, scope, acceptance, targeted remediation, and focused re-verification. Break-loop analysis also makes the causal loop explicit and requires newly discovered neighboring defects to receive an owner, target, risk, and acceptance decision instead of silently expanding the task.
