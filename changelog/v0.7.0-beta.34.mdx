New independent-evidence briefs and FIFO queues require a task-owned unit plan,
workload rationale, and grouping rationale for multiple scope items. FIFO claims
cannot skip earlier unclaimed units. The bundled Channel skill exposes one-host
multi-target dispatch, acceptance before rolling refill, and drain before a
temporary task switch. Inline mode still permits explicitly authorized Channel
evidence; required independent reports cannot be replaced by coordinator claims.

Native `task.py select` binds context without starting. Planned/change-bearing
start requires a sealed current material revision and later approval recorded
through native `task.py plan seal/approve`; replan invalidates that approval.
The record checks structure, not chat authenticity. Classify legacy planning
tasks once before start; existing in-progress tasks and historical reports stay
readable. Minor progress or wording edits do not require reapproval.

Channel CLI dispatch now lets `--profile` imply the subnode role and inherits its Codex provider. Author `--as` defaults to `TRELLIS_CHANNEL_AS` or `main`; worker IDs and recipients stay explicit.

Upgrade the paired CLI/core packages, run `trellis update`, and refresh the
selected Marketplace workflow through its immutable source ref. No bulk history
migration, new scheduler, or repeated startup on ordinary continue is introduced.
