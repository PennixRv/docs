---
title: 'Trellis 0.7.1-beta.11'
description: '2026-11-04'
---

Session handoff ownership is now a one-time transfer: `consume` completes the transfer and fixes the ownership generation. Ordinary task edits, commits, phase progress, and delivery closeout no longer rewrite the handoff package or advance its generation after consumption.

The consumed-target `transfer reconcile` operation has been removed. Sealed-source recovery before `claim` remains available through `--expected-continuity-digest`, and `transfer status --include-current` remains read-only. The CLI, bundled workflow, Pennix handoff adapter, tests, and migration guidance are aligned with this lifecycle.
